---
title: Gluten-free, milk-free brownies
date: 2026-11-09
cuisine: american
foodType: Dessert
tags:
  - Gluten-free
  - Milk-free
  - Make-ahead
servings: 24
prepTimeMinutes: 30
cookTimeMinutes: 30
ingredients: |-
  Chocolate base:
  - 273g Milk-free butter (I used Berit Smøremyk)
  - 250g 70% dark chocolate (milk-free), roughly chopped
  - 6g Espresso grounds (about 1 Nespresso pod)

  Eggs and sugar:
  - 4 Eggs
  - 4 Egg yolks
  - 380g White sugar
  - 100g Light brown sugar
  - 2 teaspoons Vanilla extract
  - 4g Fine salt

  Dry and chunks:
  - 60g Cocoa powder
  - 110g Gluten-free flour blend (I used Møllerens Fin Melblanding glutenfri)
  - 150g 70% dark chocolate (milk-free), cut into 1cm chunks
---
1. Heat the oven to 160°C fan. Grease a high-walled 22 × 32cm tray and line it with parchment, leaving overhangs to lift the brownies out by

2. Melt the butter with the chopped chocolate over low heat, keeping it below about 50°C. Stir in the coffee grounds and leave until lukewarm

3. Whisk the eggs, yolks, vanilla, both sugars and the salt in a stand mixer until pale, thick and ribbony, with no gritty sugar when you rub some between your fingers. Takes 4-5 minutes

4. With the mixer on low, stream in the chocolate mixture and mix until glossy and streak-free, about 1 minute

5. Sift in the cocoa and flour and fold by hand until no streaks remain. Fold gently so you don't knock out the air

6. Fold in the chocolate chunks

7. Leave the batter to rest for 15 minutes

8. Spread it evenly in the tray and tap the tray on the counter

9. Bake until the edges have pulled in, the top is matte and crackled and the centre still has a slight wobble. Around 30 minutes, and the centre should read 90-93°C. Check from 28 minutes

10. Cool in the tray for 10 minutes, then lift out on the parchment onto a rack and leave until completely cool. Cutting them warm gives you a soft, pasty centre

11. Cut into 24 pieces with a hot knife

Texture gets better as it cools, so ideally let it cool at room temperature for 3-4 hours before serving. Wrap leftovers airtight. They stay moist in the fridge for at least 2 days.

If you don't need it milk-free or gluten-free, normal butter, plain flour and any 70% dark chocolate should work. Use 250g unsalted butter and 6.5g salt, and the same weight of flour. I haven't tried it.
